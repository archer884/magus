# How to play Magus

You're a wizard dueling another wizard. Each of you starts at **20 life**.
Bring your opponent to 0 and you win. You also win if they need to draw a card
and their deck is empty.

## Your cards

- **Lands** (Meadow, Lagoon, Bog, Crag, Thicket) make mana, the resource you
  spend on everything else. Each makes one mana of its color: white (W),
  blue (U), black (B), red (R) or green (G). **You can play one land per turn.**
  Lands stay out, so you can afford bigger things as the game goes on.
- **Creatures** stay on the battlefield and fight. `3/2` means 3 power (the
  damage it deals) and 2 toughness (the damage it can take before it dies).
  Damage wears off at the end of each turn.
- **Sorceries** are one-shot effects you can cast only on your own turn, when
  nothing else is happening.
- **Instants** are one-shot effects you can cast at almost any time, even
  during your opponent's turn or in response to their spells.

A cost like `{2}{R}` means "two mana of any color, plus one red." The game
taps your lands for you.

## A turn

1. **Untap.** Your tapped (used) lands and creatures get ready again.
2. **Draw** a card.
3. **Main phase.** Play a land, and cast creatures and sorceries.
4. **Combat.** Choose which of your creatures attack. Your opponent chooses
   which of their creatures block. An unblocked attacker hits the opponent.
   A blocked attacker and its blocker hit each other instead.
   - Attacking **taps** a creature, so it can't block on your opponent's turn.
   - A creature can't attack the turn it arrives ("summoning sick") unless it has **haste**.
5. **Second main phase.** Cast more things after you've seen how combat went.
6. **End.** If you hold more than 7 cards, discard down to 7.

## Responding: the stack

When anyone casts a spell, it doesn't happen right away. It goes on the
**stack**, and the other player gets a chance to respond with an instant.
Responses happen first, so the last thing cast resolves first. Example: your
opponent casts Blaze at your Thornback Boar. You respond with Wild Growth
(+3/+3). Wild Growth resolves first, your Boar is now 6/6, and Blaze's 3
damage doesn't kill it.

When it's your chance to act, you have **priority**. Press Space to pass and
let things happen.

## Keywords

| | |
|---|---|
| **Flying** | Can only be blocked by creatures with flying or reach |
| **Reach** | Can block fliers |
| **Haste** | Can attack the turn it comes out |
| **Vigilance** | Doesn't tap when it attacks, so it can still block next turn |
| **Lifelink** | Damage it deals also gains you that much life |
| **Deathtouch** | Any damage it deals to a creature kills it |
| **Defender** | Can't attack |

## Tips for a first game

- Play a land every turn if you can.
- Early on, cast whatever you can afford each turn. Unused mana is wasted.
- Before you attack, ask "what if they block?" A 2/2 attacking into a 3/3 just dies.
- Keep instant-speed removal (Blaze, Grasp of Ruin) for creatures that matter.
- **Ember & Thorn** is the most straightforward deck: attack early, burn their blockers.
